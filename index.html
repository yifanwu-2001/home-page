Research Interests

Tactile Perception and Real-Time Estimation for Contact-Rich Manipulation: fabricating visuotactile sensor hardware, reconstructing contact geometry from it, and building uncertainty-aware estimators and controllers that let a robot decide when partial, noisy contact information is enough to act. Interested in extending this to mobile manipulation and imitation learning in unstructured, contact-heavy environments such as construction and field robotics, where perception is unreliable and contact is unavoidable.
